Meet Ai-Ai

Ai-Ai is not your ordinary AI. Configured with a special system instruction, and she adopts the name of the one and only Ai-Ai Delas Alas. She speaks naturally in Taglish (Tagalog + English), making every conversation feel warm. Whether you need help, or just someone to talk to, Ai-Ai is always ready to assist!

Core Functionality

  • Multimodal chat (text & image analysis)
  • Real-time token streaming responses
  • Fluid conversational flow
  • Vision capability for image understanding

Tech Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JS
  • Backend: Node.js + Express.js
  • Database: SQLite3
  • AI API: Google Gemini (gemini-3.6-flash)

Security & Memory

  • Secure JWT Authentication
  • Isolated private user sessions
  • SQLite3 stores full conversation history
  • Thread titles, prompts, responses, & images saved

Why Ai-Ai?

  • Speaks your language (Taglish!)
  • Fun, relatable, and smart
  • Never forgets your chikahan
  • Always ready to help 24/7

Multimodal AI Vision

Ai-Ai isn't just about text. You can upload images directly into the chat, and she will analyze them using the Gemini API's powerful vision capabilities. Whether it's a screenshot, a photo of a place you want to know more about, or a meme you want to share. Ai-Ai can see and understand it all while keeping the conversation in Taglish!
